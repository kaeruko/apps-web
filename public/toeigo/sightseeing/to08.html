EXPLORE THE ROUTE: NIPPORI ↔ KINSHICHO

Toei Bus 都08 (T08)

A selected sightseeing guide to some of the neighborhoods and views along TO-08. The route serves Asakusa, the Tokyo Skytree Station Entrance area and Kinshicho on its way across eastern Tokyo.

1

Nippori

A quieter side of Tokyo, with local streets and an easy gateway into the city's northeast.

2

Asakusa

Historic streets, temple-town atmosphere and one of Tokyo's most recognizable old-town districts.

3

Tokyo Skytree area

TO-08 stops at Tokyo Skytree Sta. Entrance, putting the landmark within easy reach.

4

Kinshicho

A lively east-Tokyo hub where shopping streets, everyday city life and skyline views come together.

Simplified sightseeing highlights map — not a complete stop list.
+

Street-level Tokyo

Between the landmarks, the route is full of ordinary neighborhoods and everyday city scenes you might miss on the train.
