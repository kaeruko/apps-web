Tokyo travel with Toei transportation

Explore Tokyo with Toei buses and subways.

Toei de GO is a trip-planning app focused on Tokyo's Toei Bus and Toei Subway network. Search routes, follow your ride, and make getting around Tokyo easier.

View on the App Store View on Google Play

Made for getting around Tokyo

Useful when you want to travel mainly by Toei transportation instead of relying on JR or private railways.

Route search

Find routes centered on Toei Bus and Toei Subway services.

Ride guidance

Keep track of your trip while you are on the move.

Discover places

Use the app as a starting point for outings and destinations around Tokyo.

Please note: Toei de GO is an independent app and is not an official app of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government or the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation.
